## 1. Audio Mixing & Sample Generation

### `sound_render_frame`
#### **Description**
The function [`sound_render_frame`](../../sound.c#L161) mixes discrete synthesis channels, noise generators, and TMS3615 organ tones into 16-bit PCM audio buffers.
